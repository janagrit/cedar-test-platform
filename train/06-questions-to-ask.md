# Questions to ask them

Pick 3–4 based on who you're talking to. Always have at least 2 ready.

## About the team and the work
- What does the Platform Testing team own today, and what do product teams own?
- What's the biggest pain point in testing right now: speed, flakiness, environments, or something else?
- How long does CI take on a typical PR, and what would "good" look like?
- How do you handle flaky tests today? Is there a quarantine process?
- Do you use contract testing between services? Pact, OpenAPI, or something else?
- What's the test stack? (Playwright? pytest? which CI?)
- How do you create test data without using PHI?

## About success in the role
- What would you expect me to have done in the first 30 / 60 / 90 days?
- How do you measure if the platform team is doing well?
- How does the team get product teams to adopt new tools or practices?

## About the team and culture
- How big is the team, and who would I work with most?
- How are priorities decided for the platform team?
- What do you enjoy most about working at Cedar?

## Closing
- Is there anything in my background that makes you unsure about my fit? (Gives you a chance to answer it now.)
- What are the next steps in the process?

## Don't ask in early rounds
Salary, vacation, or anything easily found on their website.
